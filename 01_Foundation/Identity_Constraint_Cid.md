# Identity Constraint ($C_{id}$)

## 1. Non-Circular Formulation

$C_{id}$ is mathematically defined not as a metaphysical identity, but as a **topological boundary operator and index generator** $\hat{C}_{\mathrm{top}}$ that classifies admissible topological charges:

$$\hat{C}_{\mathrm{top}} |\psi\rangle = q_i |\psi\rangle, \quad q_i \in \mathbb{Z} \text{ or } \pi_k(\mathcal{M})$$

The identity subspace is the invariant kernel:
$$\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{id}} = \ker(\mathbb{I} - \Pi_{C_{id}})$$

## 2. Participation in State Reduction

$C_{id}$ restricts the configuration manifold dimension:
$$\dim(\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{id}}) \ll \dim(\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{raw}})$$

This prevents trajectory divergence in the emergent relational regime $R$.
